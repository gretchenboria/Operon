ENGINEERING SCOPE & VALUE PROPOSITION
What Operon Does, Why It Is Useful, and Its Exact Technical Boundaries
1. Side-by-Side 6-DOF Sim vs. 6-DOF Real/Watch Twin (Default Mode)
Left rig computes analytical 6-DOF inverse kinematics (q1..q6) with zero phase lag. Right rig applies a physical actuator twin model (22 ms servo time constant, 0.35 deg harmonic-drive backlash deadband, gravity cantilever deflection, and live forearm IMU perturbations). You can also switch to a Single 6-DOF view or the 4-DOF vs 6-DOF planar-constraint baseline in the top bar.
2. Concrete Engineering Outputs You Can Use Today
- Extrinsic IMU Calibration (Wahba/SVD): Computes the 3x3 rotation matrix R_watch_to_sim aligning an off-the-shelf Apple Watch or phone strapped to a robot link against the kinematic link frame, plus RMSE, MAE, and Pearson r.
- Singularity-Free 6-DOF Wrist Control: Dual-branch ZYZ spherical wrist solver prevents 180 deg wrist flips near q5 = 0.
- Standard Trajectory & Dataset Exports: Unrestricted export of UTC-synced sim_imu.csv + watch.csv, LeRobot .jsonl episodes, OpenUSD .usda articulations, and 2D/3D bounding boxes.
3. Honest Scope Limitations (What This Is Not)
Operon is a browser-based kinematic, trajectory, and inertial calibration workbench. It does not run a full frictional rigid-body contact solver (such as MuJoCo or PhysX GPU contact dynamics). Use it for pre-hardware coordinate frame verification, teleop latency testing, polynomial trajectory profiling, and wearable IMU calibration.
